PhD researcher, Institute of Geosciences, Kiel University

Advised by Prof. Dr.-Ing. Frank Wuttke, StructMonitor project

I work on scientific machine learning and neural solvers for differential equations, with a focus on elastic waves in concrete. The aim is to use measured waves to find hidden damage in structures, using physics-informed neural networks and full waveform inversion.

Before Kiel, I completed an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Genoa, with a thesis on physics-informed neural networks for wave dynamics supervised by Prof. Frank Wuttke and Prof. Lorenzo Rosasco.

SUBMITTED 2026MANUSCRIPT

Adaptive Physics-Informed Neural Networks for Elastic Wave Propagation in Heterogeneous Media

A. H. Mustajab, F. Wuttke, A. Riaz Khan, Z. H. Rizvi

PirateNet reaches 1.67% error on the filtered Marmousi2 model, against about 20% for a standard PINN.

Key results
  • Filtered Marmousi2: 1.67% time-averaged relative L2 error for PirateNet 3 × 128, compared with 20.71% for the standard PINN and 18.25% for the Fourier-feature PINN.
  • Six-layer medium: 0.40% for PirateNet 2 × 128 at a similar parameter count, compared with 4.12% and 5.50%.
  • Error at the final time on Marmousi2: 3.0%, compared with 45% and 35%.

One run per configuration in two-dimensional, constant-density media with smoothed or low-pass-filtered material models. These are numerical benchmarks, not field measurements on concrete. This is a submitted manuscript, not yet peer reviewed.

APPLIED SCIENCES 2024JOURNAL

Physics-Informed Neural Networks for High-Frequency and Multi-Scale Problems Using Transfer Learning

A. H. Mustajab, H. Lyu, Z. Rizvi, F. Wuttke · Applied Sciences 14(8), 3204

Train on an easier problem first, then fine-tune: 0.037% error against 0.80% when training from scratch.

Key results
  • Damped oscillator at ω = 40: 0.037% relative L2 error with transfer learning, compared with 0.80% for Adam from scratch (Table 1).
  • 1D wave equation at c = 1.5: a loss of order 10-5 in 600 epochs with transfer learning, compared with 1,000 epochs without (Figure 9g).
  • 1D wave equation at c = 1: 0.05% relative L2 error against the analytical solution (Section 3.2).

Numerical benchmark results, not concrete damage-detection accuracy. Epoch counts do not measure end-to-end runtime.
