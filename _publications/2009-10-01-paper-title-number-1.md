This comprehensive study compares various deep learning architectures for solving full-waveform inversion (FWI) problems in geophysics. We evaluate the performance of convolutional neural networks, recurrent neural networks, and transformer-based models on synthetic and field datasets. Our analysis reveals that hybrid architectures combining physics-informed constraints with data-driven learning achieve the best results in terms of convergence speed and solution accuracy. The findings provide valuable insights for the development of next-generation FWI algorithms.
