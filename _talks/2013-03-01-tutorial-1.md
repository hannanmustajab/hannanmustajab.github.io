Half-day tutorial covering the fundamentals of operator learning and its applications in geophysics. Topics included neural operator architectures, physics-informed constraints, and hands-on implementation of wave propagation models using PyTorch. Participants learned to build and train neural operators for solving forward and inverse problems in geophysics.
