HI, I'M HANNAN

Hannan

I'm a PhD researcher at Kiel University. My research explores how neural networks can learn physics well enough to model what we cannot easily measure.

That means waves travelling through the ground and through concrete, ice forming in the pores of frozen soil, and vibrations that reveal damage in structures.

What I work on

ADVISED BY PROF. DR.-ING. FRANK WUTTKE
01 Waves in the ground and in concrete Elastic and acoustic wave modelling in geophysical domains and in heterogeneous structures such as concrete. WAVE PHYSICS
02 Seeing damage without breaking things Non-destructive testing and structural health monitoring, using full waveform inversion to look inside structures. NDT, SHM
03 Machine learning that respects the physics Physics-informed neural networks with lattice element modelling and the spectral element method, and diffusion models to guide ill-posed inverse problems. SCIML, LEM, SEM, DIFFUSION
04 How soil behaves Soil interaction modelling, including our current project on frozen soil, where we use GNO to model pore interaction. GEOMECHANICS, FROZEN SOIL, GNO
