This work investigates the application of operator learning methods to wave propagation problems in heterogeneous geological media. We propose a novel neural operator architecture that can efficiently learn the mapping between source parameters and wavefield solutions, significantly reducing computational costs compared to traditional finite-difference methods. The approach is validated on realistic geological models and shows promise for real-time seismic imaging applications.
